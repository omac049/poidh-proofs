Rig the vote

BLOCKED

Once others chip in, the issuer can't just pay a claim. It goes to a vote of everyone who funded the bounty, weighted by what they put in: the issuer's stake starts as yes, and contributors who put in more can vote it down. Weights are snapshotted when the vote starts, joining or leaving is frozen until it's settled, and after two days anyone can settle it.

resolveVote is callable by anyone after the deadline. After it, resetVotingPeriod reverts with VoteWouldPass if the claim won.

8 levers pulled. The bounty is still open.

So who can stop it? The honest list is short:

  • The issuer can cancel it while no claim is accepted and no vote is running. That returns the issuer's own ETH; anyone who chipped in takes theirs back too. It's their money.
  • A contributor can take back their own share when no vote is running, never anyone else's.
  • poidh.xyz can hide it from poidh.xyz.
  • Base's sequencer, run by Coinbase, could delay transactions. OP Stack chains let anyone force a transaction in through Ethereum.

A government, a payment processor, a platform's trust and safety team, poidh itself: none of them has a function to call.

Freedom tech isn't a company promising not to press the censor button. It's a contract with no censor button to press.
